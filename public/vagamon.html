RISK ASSESSMENT — VAGAMON JULY 2026
😴
Fatigue — Sleep Deprivation
🚨 HIGH
📅 Day 0 (22:00–02:00) + Day 1 (03:00–07:40) · Only 4 hours sleep before 280km ghat ride
Sleep by 22:00 sharp. Set 2 alarms. No late night scrolling.
If you feel heavy eyes at any point — PULL OVER. Even 15 min sleep in a petrol station is fine.
Coffee only at Thrissur departure, not before sleep. Don't mask tiredness with caffeine on ghat.
Deva should watch for Kabi's micro-sleep signs: wandering lane, slow reaction at curves.
🌫️
Fog + Zero Visibility
🚨 HIGH
📅 Day 1 · 03:00–07:00 · Ghat approach and ghat section
Reduce speed immediately when fog appears. 20 km/h on ghat if visibility < 30m.
Use low-beam in fog (high-beam reflects off fog and blinds you further).
Horn at every blind curve — one long honk.
Pull over and wait if fog is extremely dense. There is no shame in stopping.
🌧️
Wet & Slippery Roads
🚨 HIGH
📅 All days · Ghat section most critical · July = peak monsoon
Sintered brake pads fitted before trip (already in bike checklist).
On wet road: brake earlier, slower entry speed on corners, no hard throttle mid-corner.
Rain gear must be accessible — not buried in saddle bag. Keep it in the top box or jacket.
Avoid painted road markings and metal surfaces — extremely slippery when wet.
⏰
Timeline Overrun
⚠️ MEDIUM
📅 Day 1 afternoon · Activities may run beyond plan
Hard rule: start return ride Day 2 before 12:00. No exceptions.
If Day 1 activities overrun → skip Pine Forest, prioritize rest for Day 2 trek.
Day 2 return: if delayed past 14:00 departure → consider overnight stay rather than night riding home.
