RISK REGISTER
🌧️
Monsoon Rain — Ghat Sections
HIGH
When: Day 2 Chinnar–Munnar section & Day 4 return · July is peak monsoon
Rain gear on before entering ghats — not mid-ride
Reduce speed to 30–40 km/h in heavy rain
Pull over and wait if visibility drops below 50m
Check tyres — wet ghats demand 100% tread
🐘
Wildlife Crossing — Chinnar–Marayoor Zone
HIGH
When: Early morning Day 2 (04:00–08:00) and Day 4 return
Do not use full beam at corners — blind animals
Speed limit 20–30 km/h through Chinnar sanctuary zone
If elephant spotted — stop engine, wait, do not honk
Dawn and dusk are peak wildlife movement times — stay alert
🌫️
Low Visibility — Fog at Altitude
MEDIUM
When: Kolukkumalai early morning (05:00) and high altitude roads
Use low beam — not high beam in fog
Wear warm layers — cold at 2100m even in July
Stay behind local jeeps on narrow trails if possible
🛣️
Road Conditions — Ghat Hairpins
MEDIUM
When: Chinnar → Munnar ascent and Munnar → Marayoor descent
Downshift before corners — do not brake mid-turn on wet road
Horn at blind turns — vehicles come fast from opposite side
Keep left — not middle of road on hairpins
Take rest breaks on long descents — brake fade risk
⛽
Fuel Availability
LOW
When: Chinnar to Munnar stretch has limited fuel stations
Full tank from Pollachi before Day 2 departure
Top up at Udumalpet (05:45 stop)
Munnar has fuel — do not stress on this
📵

LOW
